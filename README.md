# Tours Alegría website package (toursalegria.com)

Last updated October 2026.

## What's here
This folder is your whole website. `index.html` is the English home page and `es/index.html` is the Spanish one.
Each tour has its own page (for example `tamanique-waterfalls-tour.html`), with a Spanish copy inside `es/`.
Your photos live under `assets/images/`, and your colors and fonts live in one file, `assets/css/tokens.css`.

## Send us your photos
Drop your originals into `_source/photos-original/` (and your logo into `_source/logos-original/`).
Any format works, we convert them. This is what we still need:

| What | Where it appears | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| 2 to 4 photos of each tour (people enjoying it, not only the place) | Tour cards and each tour page | horizontal, min 1600px wide |
| Your logo | Header, footer, browser icon | vector (SVG, AI or PDF) if possible |
| The van with its stickers (the AI mock-up is fine) | "Our story" section | horizontal |
| A half-body photo of each of you, relaxed | "Our story" section | min 1200px |

Tip: name each tour folder in Google Drive with the tour name so we can match them fast.

## Change your colors
All colors and fonts are in `assets/css/tokens.css`. Editing that one file re-skins the whole site.
